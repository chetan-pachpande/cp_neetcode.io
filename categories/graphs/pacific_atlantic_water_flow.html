Pacific Atlantic Water Flow

Reverse DFS from oceans ⏱ O(mn) time 💾 O(mn) space

Problem

Water can flow from a cell to adjacent cells with height less than or equal to the current height. Return all coordinates where water can flow to both the Pacific and Atlantic oceans.

The Pacific touches the top and left edges. The Atlantic touches the bottom and right edges.

Input: 1,2,2,3,5 / 3,2,3,4,4 / 2,4,5,3,1 / 6,7,1,4,5 / 5,1,1,2,4
Output: cells reachable by both oceans

Intuition

Checking every cell by flowing downhill to oceans is repetitive. Reverse the direction: start from each ocean edge and climb uphill or flat.

If an ocean can climb from its edge to a cell, then water from that cell can flow back down to that ocean. Intersect Pacific-reachable and Atlantic-reachable cells.

Full Explanation

The trick is reversing the flow. Instead of dropping water from every mountain cell and asking whether it reaches an ocean, we let each ocean climb inward. From an ocean edge, the search may move to a neighbor only when that neighbor is at least as tall as the current cell.

That reversed move means water could flow downhill from the neighbor back to the ocean. After running this search from Pacific edges and Atlantic edges, cells marked by both searches are exactly the answer.
